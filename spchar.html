HTML Special Characters

HTML Special Characters

Entity: A mechanism for writing special characters or symbols in HTML, such as math symbols, characters in non-Latin scripts, and other symbols. Many HTML entities can be specified by name, decimal number, or by a hexadecimal number.

Example: A copyright logo
© - &copy;

© - &#169;

© - &#x000A9;


Emojis

Emojis can be displayed in a webpage with a decimal or hexadecimal entity. Example: The happy face: 😀 - 😀

Entities for reserved characters

The < and & characters can be displayed using entities, &lt; for less than, &amp; for the ampersand, EX: 2 < 3

Non-Breaking Characters

A non-breaking character is an inter-word character that permits treating the words on both sides to be one word

Non-Breaking Hyphens

A non-breaking hyphen ‑, looks like a regular hyphen but acts like a normal character in the middle of the word. Non‑Breaking Hyphens

Non-Breaking Space

A non-breaking space  , looks like a single space but acts like a normal space. This is my wo  rd.

WARNING: - Do NOT use non-breaking spaces to create spaces for the layout of your page
